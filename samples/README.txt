Put your own photos here (they are never committed: .gitignore skips every image file in samples/).

Intended photos for the demo:
  street.jpg         vertical elevated street, many parked cars, several clearly visible pedestrians,
                     bikes, a taxi. Main photo (people + cars, occlusion for NMS, portrait aspect stresses the letterbox).
  street_cars.jpg    aerial view of parked cars and vans, good light, no people: a clean "cars only" backup.
                     Plates are legible: blur them before putting it on a public page or repo.
  street_shops.jpg   storefronts, one big car, two small distant pedestrians; people are weak here, use a lower threshold.

You must have the right to use every photo. Credit the sources. Any .jpg/.jpeg/.png/.webp file in this folder shows up as a thumbnail.
After adding photos run scripts/record_results.py to create the offline "Recorded" data for them.
